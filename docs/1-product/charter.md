# Asgard product charter

Direction for Asgard, the company factory, within the
[Ouroboric charter](https://github.com/ouroboric-dev/ouroboric/blob/main/docs/1-company/charter.md).

## Vision

Starting a company or a product is as deliberate and checkable as building software: direction is
written down, agreed and tested before anything is built.

## Mission

Turn an intent into a ready-to-build company or product, with a charter, brand seed, legal basics,
roadmap and first design doc, and prove it is ready with an honest readiness assessment.

## Principles

Asgard follows the Ouroboric principles. Product-specific:

1. **Direction is human.** Asgard drafts and checks; people decide. Charter approval stops for a human
   in every mode by default.
2. **Readiness is evidence.** Every readiness item links to evidence or is marked missing; nothing is
   assumed.
3. **Asgard is not a lawyer.** It scaffolds legal material and flags what needs a qualified person; it
   never presents its output as legal advice.

## Users and problems

Operators starting a company or product (first: Ouroboric itself, then products such as a client
gallery for photographers). The problem: new ventures start building before direction, legal ground
and brand are settled, and pay for it later.

## North-star measures

- Readiness gaps found before building rather than after.
- Time from intent to an accepted charter and first design doc.

## Boundaries

- Runs on Yggdrasil's engine (Odin); no engine of its own.
- v0 is templates, the readiness checklist and the documented manual kickstart; automation (skills,
  scripts, the Ginnungagap graph) comes after Yggdrasil v0.
