# Asgard product charter

> **Superseded on 2026-10-17.** Asgard is no longer a separate factory: it is Yggdrasil's kickstart
> realm. Its direction now lives in the
> [Yggdrasil charter](https://github.com/ouroboric-dev/yggdrasil/blob/main/docs/1-product/charter.md)
> and the Asgard v0 design doc in Yggdrasil's `docs/1-product/design/`. This repository becomes a
> leaf of `yggdrasil` after Yggdrasil v0. The text below is kept as the record of the original
> direction.

This charter sets direction for Asgard, the company factory, within the
[Ouroboric charter](https://github.com/ouroboric-dev/ouroboric/blob/main/docs/1-company/charter.md).

## Vision

Starting a company or a product is as deliberate and checkable as building software: direction is
written down, agreed and tested before anything is built.

## Mission

Turn an intent into a ready-to-build company or product, with a charter, brand seed, legal basics,
roadmap and first design doc; prove it is ready with an honest readiness assessment; and learn from
every kickstart to make the next one better.

## Principles

Asgard follows the Ouroboric principles and adds three of its own:

1. **Direction is human.** Asgard drafts and checks; people decide. Charter approval stops for a human
   in every mode by default.
2. **Readiness is evidence.** Every readiness item links to evidence or is marked missing; nothing is
   assumed.
3. **Asgard is not a lawyer.** It scaffolds legal material and flags what needs a qualified person; it
   never presents its output as legal advice.

## Users and problems

Asgard serves operators starting a company or product: first Ouroboric itself, then products such as
a client gallery for photographers. It addresses one problem: new ventures start building before they
settle direction, legal ground and brand, and pay for it later.

## North-star measures

- Readiness gaps found before building rather than after.
- Time from intent to an accepted charter and first design doc.
- Fewer readiness gaps found after building, kickstart over kickstart: every late gap becomes a
  checklist item for the next venture.

## Boundaries

- Runs on Yggdrasil's engine (Odin); no engine of its own.
- Open source: AGPL-3.0 for Asgard's code, MIT for templates copied into the companies and products
  it kickstarts, CC BY 4.0 for docs. What it produces belongs to the operator.
- v0 is templates, the readiness checklist and the documented manual kickstart; automation (skills,
  scripts, the Ginnungagap graph) comes after Yggdrasil v0.
