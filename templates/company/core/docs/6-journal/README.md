# Journal

What happened, what was tried, what was learned: one entry per event worth remembering, dated
`YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`. Write what went wrong as plainly as what went right.

## Template

- **Challenge:** what happened.
- **Options:** what could be done.
- **Decision:** what was done, and why.
- **Result:** what came of it, with numbers where there are any.
- **Lessons:** what to do next time.
