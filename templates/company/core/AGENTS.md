## Invariants

1. Direction is human. Draft and check the charter, the brand and the roadmap; never approve them. The founder
   approves by merging a pull request.
2. Never read, print or store secrets, passwords, tokens or keys. The accounts inventory records where recovery
   details are kept, never the details.
3. Not a lawyer. Scaffold legal and admin material and flag what needs a qualified person; never present it as
   legal, tax or financial advice.
4. Readiness is evidence. Link every readiness item to its evidence, or mark it missing.
5. Work on a branch and open a pull request for every change.
