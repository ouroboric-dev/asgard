# Ways of working

{{COMPANY}} builds its products with a software factory, and follows the standards that factory works by. They
are linked, not copied, so nothing here drifts from them; the link to each one, at the version the company
follows, is in `.ouroboric/standards.yaml`.

- **Spec-driven development:** how work goes from an idea to a merged change, and who approves what.
- **Docs standard:** where each document lives, and its front matter.
- **Writing styles:** how each kind of document is written.
- **Diagrams:** how diagrams are drawn and rendered.

The company's own rules, beyond these, are in [rules.md](rules.md).
