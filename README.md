# Asgard

The company factory by [Ouroboric](https://github.com/ouroboric-dev/ouroboric): it turns an intent
into a ready-to-build company or product (charter, brand seed, legal basics, roadmap, first design
doc) and proves readiness with an honest assessment. It runs on the engine of
[Yggdrasil](https://github.com/ouroboric-dev/yggdrasil), the software factory.

- [Product charter](docs/1-product/charter.md)
- [Readiness checklist](templates/readiness/checklist.md)
- Kickstart process: [spec-driven development, tier 0](https://github.com/ouroboric-dev/ouroboric/blob/main/docs/4-ways-of-working/spec-driven-development.md#31-tier-0-kickstarting-a-company-or-a-product)

v0 (after Yggdrasil v0): templates, the readiness checklist and the documented manual kickstart.
Later: kickstart skills (in Havamal), scripts, and the Ginnungagap graph on Odin.
